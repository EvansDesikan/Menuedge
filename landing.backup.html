Menu Engineering Matrix

Every dish is classified using the proven Stars / Plowhorses / Puzzles / Dogs framework — the same methodology taught at Cornell's School of Hotel Administration. Know exactly what to promote, reprice, and remove.

Stars — keep & promote Puzzles — boost visibility Plowhorses — reprice Dogs — redesign or cut
Star
High popularity, high margin. Keep visible and promote actively.
Plowhorse
Guests love it, but margin is thin. Bundle or reprice.
Puzzle
Great margin, low orders. Rename, rewrite, reposition.
Dog
Low popularity, low margin. Redesign or remove entirely.
